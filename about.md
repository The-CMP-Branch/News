# About The CMP Branch — RISC OS News

This site curates news and updates from the RISC OS community, bringing together announcements, software releases, hardware developments, and community events in one place.

## Editorial Policy

- **Community-focused**: We cover news relevant to RISC OS users, developers, and enthusiasts.
- **Source attribution**: Every article credits its original source with a link where available.
- **Accuracy**: We strive to share accurate information. Spotted a mistake? [Report a correction](https://github.com/The-CMP-Branch/News/issues/new?template=correction.yml).
- **No editorial content**: This is a news aggregation and curation site. Opinion pieces and analysis are attributed to their original authors.

## About RISC OS

RISC OS is a computer operating system originally designed by Acorn Computers Ltd in Cambridge, England. First released in 1987, it was designed to run on the ARM chipset and continues to be developed and used by a dedicated community today.

## Stay Updated

## Suggest a News Item

Know about something happening in the RISC OS world that we haven't covered? A new release, hardware announcement, show, or community project? Tell us by opening an issue on GitHub:

[Suggest a news item →](https://github.com/The-CMP-Branch/News/issues/new?template=news-suggestion.yml){: .btn}

You don't need to write the article. Just tell us what the news is and link to where it was announced; we'll check the source and write it up. Suggestions should be RISC OS related and come from a public source such as a forum post, mailing list, product page, or press release. It's also worth checking the [open suggestions](https://github.com/The-CMP-Branch/News/issues?q=is%3Aissue+is%3Aopen+label%3Anews-suggestion) first in case someone has beaten you to it.

You'll need a free GitHub account to open an issue.
