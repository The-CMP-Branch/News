Chris Hall is the featured speaker at tonight's Bristol RISC OS User Group meeting, talking about ARM 64. He posted in the [RISC OS Open Announcements forum](https://www.riscosopen.org/forum/forums/1/topics/20669) on 12 November 2026 that there is a meeting of the group tonight, and as a taster for what he will say he shared a screenshot from what he calls his RISC OS 64 machine.

[The Icon Bar](https://www.iconbar.com/articles/Chris_Hall_will_be_talking_ARM_64_at_tonights_Bristol_RISC_OS_User_Group/index2422.html), in a piece by Mark Stephens the same day, reports that Hall is the featured speaker at the November meeting and that he will have lots of hardware to talk about.

## Meeting details

The gathering is on Thursday 12th November at around 7:00pm at The Open Arms on St Michael's Hill, Bristol. This site's [mid-November meetings round-up]({{ '/2026/11/07/risc-os-user-group-meetings-mid-november-2026/' | relative_url }}) had listed it as an informal pub meet with no guest speakers. The Icon Bar notes that the evening remains very informal and that people can just show up.

Bristol RISC OS Users (BRU) publish further details on the [group website](http://bristol.riscosusers.org.uk/), including the [meetings page](http://bristol.riscosusers.org.uk/meetings.html), which lists 12 November 2026 among the group's likely dates for the year.

## RISC OS 64 taster

Hall says he will bring the machine to the pub but will not plug it in. A photo of the machine shows that he has already added hardware: an SB Components USB-connected Real Time Clock, with software to control it.
