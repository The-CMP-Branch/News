A few RISC OS user groups have meetings listed for December 2026, following the gatherings covered in this site's [mid-November round-up]({{ '/2026/11/07/risc-os-user-group-meetings-mid-november-2026/' | relative_url }}). Dates and arrangements can change, so anyone planning to attend should check the groups' own pages and mailing lists.

## WROCC – Wednesday 2nd December

Wakefield RISC OS Computer Club's next meeting is on Wednesday 2nd December 2026. [WROCC's meetings page](https://www.wrocc.org.uk/meetings) lists Andrew Youll talking under the title *Building Blocks*: he will talk about his use of AI, his ports, original software and RISC OS on Linux.

WROCC meets online via Zoom. Meetings start at 7.45pm, and the scheduled part of the evening is generally complete by about 10pm. Visitors are welcome, except at the AGM and EGMs; contact WROCC for details.

Looking ahead, WROCC lists a talk on Wednesday 13th January 2027 — noted as the second Wednesday of the month, rather than the usual first — by Mark Stephens, *Vibe coding !Claude, a BBC BASIC application for RISC OS*. He will cover what he learnt, what went wrong, and how he would and would not use AI in future projects, with a live demonstration, and the audience will pick a new feature to add together. The annual general meeting follows on 3rd February 2027 and is members-only.

## WAUG – Saturday 12th December

The Wessex ARM User Group's next meeting is on Zoom on Saturday 12th December 2026, from 10:30am to 12:30pm, according to [WAUG's website](https://waug.tmfraser.co.uk/). The site says monthly meetings are currently held on Zoom, usually on the second Saturday of the month, and that a Google Groups mailing list is used for meeting information and changes of date.

## ROUGOL – no December meeting listed

The RISC OS User Group of London's [home page](https://www.rougol.jellybaby.net/) shows a future meetings list of 16th November (RISC OS 10 deep dive) and 18th January (TBC). There is no December meeting on that list. ROUGOL usually meets on the third Monday of each month.

## Bristol

The [Bristol RISC OS Users meetings page](http://bristol.riscosusers.org.uk/meetings.html) currently lists 2026 dates on the second Thursday of odd months, ending with 12th November, and does not show a December gathering.

RISCOSitory has not published a December meetings round-up. Its [November preview](https://www.riscository.com/2026/meetings-and-events-november-2026/) already noted WROCC on 2nd December, WAUG on the morning of the 12th, and no ROUGOL meeting scheduled for the month.

Further details are on the [WROCC meetings page](https://www.wrocc.org.uk/meetings), [WAUG's website](https://waug.tmfraser.co.uk/), and [ROUGOL's site](https://www.rougol.jellybaby.net/).
