Nick Anderson, who posts as jankfactor, has released Space Chase, a 3D space-combat game for Acorn Archimedes machines. He announced it on [StarDot](https://stardot.org.uk/forums/viewtopic.php?t=33956) on 17 November 2026. The game is a name-your-own-price download from [itch.io](https://jankfactor.itch.io/spacechase).

## Gameplay and development

Anderson wrote Space Chase in C and ARM assembly, using 3D routines he has worked on for several years. The player chases a suspect through several star systems, deals with their backup, and brings them back to base. On StarDot he said the dogfighting is partly inspired by Elite Dangerous.

He presented the game at the RISC OS User Group of London's meeting on 19 October 2026. [ROUGOL](https://www.rougol.jellybaby.net/meetings/2026/oct.html) described it as inspired by Elite's flight section but built into a more arcade-style combat game.

The release is targeted at the A3010 and A3020 and up. Anderson wrote on StarDot that he is "reasonably pleased" with A3000 performance. There is no Risc PC or StrongARM version yet: when asked on StarDot, he said he was not sure how to build one.

## Download

itch.io lists Space Chase as name-your-own-price. The download is an 800 KB ADF disc image for a Gotek or an emulator. On 19 November Anderson added a "Performance Test" ADF for A5000 alpha machines. A browser version also runs on the itch.io page, but he recommends original hardware or a dedicated emulator such as Arculator or RPCEmu.

Art, music, 3D models and sound effects were made by hand. The itch.io page is tagged "No generative AI was used". Anderson says the source code is due to go on his GitHub "at some point", and has called the release his "soft exit from the retro scene for now".

The StarDot thread has been warm. Matt Godbolt wrote: "Absolutely amazing! So great to see this finished: but now I have to get my Archie working...!"
