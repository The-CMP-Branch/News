The RISC OS User Group of London (ROUGOL) has confirmed the speaker and topic for its Monday 16th November 2026 meeting: Andrew Rawnsley of RISC OS Developments (ROD) will present a RISC OS 10 deep dive. [This site's mid-November meetings round-up]({{ '/2026/11/07/risc-os-user-group-meetings-mid-november-2026/' | relative_url }}) had noted that a ROUGOL speaker had not yet been arranged.

ROUGOL meets on the third Monday of each month. The 16th November gathering is a hybrid meeting, held in person upstairs in the Petworth Room at The Duke of Sussex public house, 23 Baylis Road, London SE1 7AY, and online via Zoom. The meeting starts at 7:45pm. Members typically begin arriving at the pub from 6.30pm, and the Zoom session opens at 7.30pm.

## RISC OS 10 after the London Show

At the RISC OS London Show on 24 October 2026, RISC OS Developments presented the 64-bit RISC OS 10 in their theatre talk. ROUGOL says the system is still clearly in an alpha state, but the company was already able to demonstrate it running real-world programs. A recording of that talk is among the [London Show theatre presentations now online]({{ '/2026/11/07/london-show-2026-theatre-talks-online/' | relative_url }}).

Because the show slot only allowed a brief demonstration, Rawnsley will use the longer meeting format for a deeper look at the new system. ROUGOL hopes — though it makes no promises — to have RISC OS 10 running in the pub so attendees can take a closer look.

## Attendance

Meetings are free and everyone is welcome; no booking is required. In-person visitors can simply turn up at the pub. People joining on Zoom should [contact ROUGOL](https://www.rougol.jellybaby.net/contacts/index.html) for the link, which is the same as for previous Zoom meetings.

Details are on [ROUGOL's meetings page](https://www.rougol.jellybaby.net/meetings/index.html). The group's site currently lists its next future meeting after November as 18 January (TBC).
