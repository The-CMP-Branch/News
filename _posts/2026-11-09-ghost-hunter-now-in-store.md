AMCOG Games' 25th RISC OS title, Ghost Hunter, is now on sale from !Store. [RISCOSitory reported the news on 8 November](https://www.riscository.com/2026/ghost-hunter-now-in-store/), after the game was unveiled at the RISC OS London Show on 24 October 2026. AMCOG's own [Ghost Hunter page](https://www.amcog.co.uk/ghost_hunter.htm) lists the game as available from the PlingStore from 5 November.

RISCOSitory says the !Store copy is version 1.03, and that anyone who bought the game at the show can upgrade free using the code they received at purchase. AMCOG's product page describes the download as version 1.3.

## Gameplay

You control a fire spirit summoned by the wizard Orzabal to clear his castle of ghosts and ghouls that appeared while he was travelling. The spirit is paid in gold found in the castle; Orzabal can replace it because he knows the secret of alchemy. Keys unlock doors, candles restore energy lost on contact with enemies, and cages can trap them briefly. Light has to be brought back to the castle: as the fire spirit moves, gloomy walls regain their light, and restoring all of them completes a section.

AMCOG presents the same plot on its product page, and notes that running out of energy sends the spirit back to the "dungeon dimensions", so Orzabal has to summon another.

## Features, price and compatibility

The game is priced at £14.99. Both RISCOSitory and AMCOG describe four-directional scrolling, 16 million colour SVGA graphics, high quality animation cycles, sound effects via AMCOG's RDSP, three original music tracks using 1980s analogue synthesisers, joystick support on RISC OS 5, full source code, and free updates. RISCOSitory also notes user-definable keys.

AMCOG lists compatibility with RISC OS 4.02 and RISC OS 5 on Raspberry Pi, RPCEmu, ARMX6, Titanium, Pandaboard, Beagleboard and Virtual Acorn. A Raspberry Pi 2 is the recommended minimum for Pi users; AMCOG says the game is still playable on a Pi 1.

The product page, news and a gameplay video are on [AMCOG Games](https://www.amcog.co.uk/ghost_hunter.htm). The [5 November news item](https://www.amcog-games.co.uk/news.htm) marks Ghost Hunter as the company's 25th finished RISC OS game.
