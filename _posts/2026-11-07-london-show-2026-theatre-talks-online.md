Bryan Hogan has published video recordings of the theatre talks from the RISC OS London Show held on 24th October 2026. Three presentations are now available on YouTube, capturing the main stage talks from RISC OS Developments, CJE Micro's and RISC OS Open.

The recordings are:

- [RISC OS Developments (ROD)](https://youtu.be/UY8RjeP_yww)
- [CJE Micro's](https://youtu.be/CNJcfnHglWg)
- [RISC OS Open (ROOL)](https://youtu.be/_gVj15WkQmk)

The videos allow those who could not attend the show to catch up on announcements and demonstrations from the major RISC OS hardware and software vendors, as well as the latest developments from RISC OS Open. Discussion of the recordings is open in the [forum announcement thread](https://www.riscosopen.org/forum/forums/1/topics/20651).
