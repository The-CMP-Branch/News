The Icon Bar is calling for contributions to its [2026 Advent Calendar](https://www.iconbar.com/articles/Announcing_the_TIB_2026_Advent_Calendar/index2429.html). Mark Stephens posted the request on 22 November 2026 at 10:13, and repeated it the same day in the RISC OS Open Announcements forum as [We need your help for the 2026 IconBar Advent calendar](https://www.riscosopen.org/forum/forums/1/topics/20683).

The Icon Bar has run an Advent Calendar over the festive season for the last few years. Stephens writes that it has shown up a lot of activity in the RISC OS world, including a few exclusive reveals, and that it depends entirely on community contributions. In the forum post he says the TIB editorial team will be too busy in December to write articles, so the calendar is how the gap is filled: the more items that arrive, the better it will be.

## What to send

Anything RISC OS related is welcome. Stephens says that covers a first Hello World or BBC BASIC application as much as a large project, and that if it is meaningful to the person who made it, The Icon Bar wants to feature it. Suggested examples include desktops, pet projects, new hardware or software, books, and teasers. Multiple submissions are welcome.

Previous years' calendars are collected at [The Icon Bar's Advent Calendar index](https://www.iconbar.com/articles/advent/).

Stephens jokes that if contributions are thin, readers will spend the month looking at a picture of his desktop — complete with a cat background — and his vibe-coded BBC BASIC [!Claude](https://github.com/markee174/claude-riscos-client) app.

## How to contribute

Use the contact details in [the Icon Bar article](https://www.iconbar.com/articles/Announcing_the_TIB_2026_Advent_Calendar/index2429.html). Stephens gives his address there as markstephens AT idrsolutions.com, and asks people to send anything they want to feature, or any questions.
